# story-bank.md — the reusable STAR story bank

Built once from `personal/career-profile.md`, reused for every interview. Lives at
`personal/interview-prep/story-bank.md` (gitignored). 8–12 stories is the sweet spot —
enough to cover the competency map, few enough to actually know cold.

## STAR structure (one entry)
```markdown
### {short title} — {company}
- **Competencies:** {tags — e.g., leadership, influence-without-authority, delivery-under-pressure}
- **Situation:** {the context, briefly — where/when/stakes}
- **Task:** {what you specifically owned}
- **Action:** {what YOU did — first person, specific, the decisions and trade-offs}
- **Result:** {the outcome, quantified where the profile supports it; the "so what"}
- **Follow-up ready:** {the likely probes — "your exact role?", "what would you do differently?"}
```

- **First person.** "I decided… I pushed for…" — panels probe for *your* action inside a team win.
- **Result first in your head, S-T-A-R in delivery.** Know the punchline; tell it in order.
- **Numbers only if the profile has them.** No fabrication (honesty rule). If a story is strong but the metric is a `[NEED METRIC]` gap, use it without the number and flag it to fill.
- **Keep each to ~90 seconds spoken.** Trim the Situation; interviewers want Action + Result.

## Competency checklist — cover these
Every strong interview draws from this set. Aim for at least one solid story per line;
flag any with no real story as a prep gap (prep an honest answer, don't invent one).

- **Leadership / building a team** (grew/led/hired)
- **Influence without authority** (drove change you didn't control)
- **Conflict / disagreement** (with a peer, boss, or stakeholder — and the resolution)
- **Failure / mistake** (a real one, what you learned, what changed)
- **Ambiguity / no playbook** (0→1, undefined problem)
- **Delivery under pressure** (deadline, crisis, constraint)
- **Biggest impact / proudest win** (the signature achievement)
- **Persuading up** (got an exec/board to a yes)
- **Turnaround / fixing something broken**
- **Cross-functional collaboration** (worked across orgs)
- **Data-driven decision / judgment call** (with incomplete info)
- **Handling underperformance / a hard people call** (if applicable)

## Mining the profile
Read `career-profile.md` end to end and pull the moments already flagged as SIGNATURE or
carrying real metrics — those are your best stories. One profile accomplishment often
covers several competencies (tag it with all of them). Prefer recent + high-scope for
senior roles; keep one or two early-career stories for "tell me about a time you learned X."
