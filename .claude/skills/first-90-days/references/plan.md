# plan.md — 30/60/90 structure + the file format

Calibrate to the role's **mandate** (what they hired you to do):
- **Turnaround / fix-it** → faster diagnosis, name the hard problems sooner, quick stabilizing win.
- **Scale / optimize** → learn the working machine deeply before touching it; wins come from leverage.
- **0→1 / build** → more listening to the need, then a scrappy first version; relationships across the org.
- **IC / hands-on** → deliver a real piece of work early; less org-politics, more craft + a quick ship.

## The file (`personal/applications/<company>/first-90-days.md`)
```markdown
# First 90 Days — {role} at {company}
_Mandate: {turnaround / scale / build / IC}. Drafted {date}; review with manager in week 1._

## Days 1–30 — Learn & listen
- **Business/domain:** {what to understand; the metrics you'll own; the history to learn}
- **Relationships:** {manager 1:1 + expectations; peers; team 1:1s; key stakeholders to meet}
- **Do:** {listening tour, read the docs/data, ask "why is it this way?"}. **Don't:** {big changes yet}

## Days 31–60 — Diagnose & a first win
- **Point of view:** {the read you're forming, backed by what you learned}
- **First win:** {one visible, low-risk, genuinely helpful thing to ship}
- **Align:** {check your diagnosis with your manager}

## Days 61–90 — Set direction
- **Plan/operating model:** {the direction the team + manager buy into}
- **Execute:** {what's underway; what "good at 90 days" looks like}

## Watch-outs
- {culture landmines, the predecessor's baggage, the stakeholder to win early, the trap to avoid}
```

## The four tracks (thread through all three phases)
Business & domain · Relationships (manager first) · Early wins (1–2, under-promised) · Operating rhythm.

## Manager alignment
The single highest-leverage move: get your manager's definition of success for THIS role in
week one, and shape the plan to it. "Here's my draft — what would you change?" builds trust and
de-risks the whole ramp.
