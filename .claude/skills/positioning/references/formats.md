# formats.md — the three positioning formats

All three share ONE core claim (the through-line). They differ in length and context, not
in who the person is. Ground every proof point in `personal/career-profile.md`.

## One-liner (positioning statement)
A single sentence for under-your-name / a headline / an intro. Formula:
`{what you are} who {through-line / the thing you're known for}{, for target audience}.`
- Example shape: "A data & analytics leader who turns messy, entrenched data into trusted, self-serve capability."
- Test: could a peer with a different edge say the exact same thing? If yes, sharpen it.

## 30-second pitch (elevator / networking)
~60–80 words. Arc: **who you are → the through-line → one concrete proof → what you're looking for.**
- Who: role + level in a phrase.
- Through-line: the differentiator (the *combination*, not one skill).
- Proof: one real, quantified win from the profile.
- Ask: the kind of role/next step you're after (so a listener can help).
- Conversational, in their voice — this is spoken, not read.

## 2-minute narrative ("tell me about yourself")
~150–200 words, a short story, not a résumé recital. Arc:
1. **Origin** — where you started (one line).
2. **Growth** — the through-line forming across a couple of moves (what you got good at, why).
3. **Now** — what you do today at scope, with one proof point.
4. **Next** — why this role/step is the logical, motivated next chapter (the "why," from self-assessment).
Land on the target role. Interview-prep reuses this verbatim as the opener — so make it drill-ready.

## Tailoring (flex emphasis, keep the core)
- **Startup vs. enterprise:** lead with build/0→1 and range for startups; scale, governance, and stakes for enterprise.
- **Recruiter vs. hiring manager:** recruiter → fit + motivation + level; hiring manager → the through-line + a proof that maps to their problem.
- **Warm intro vs. cold:** warm → shorter, assume context; cold → one extra line of who-you-are.
Never change the core claim — only which proof and which facet you lead with.
