# weekly-plan.md — sizing targets + the log format

## Sizing targets to capacity (a starting rubric, then tune)
| Situation | Applications/wk | Warm touches/wk | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Employed, discreet search** | 2–4 (tailored) | 2–3 | Protect energy; quality + network over volume |
| **Full-time searching** | 5–8 (tailored) | 5–8 | More capacity, but still tailored — not spray |
| **Urgent (short runway)** | 8–10 | 8–10 + events | Add channels; widen lane slightly; keep prep sharp |
| **Passive / exploring** | 0–2 | 1–2 | Mostly network + roadmap moves; watch for the right role |

Adjust for what the pipeline needs: thin top-of-funnel → more applications/outreach; interviews
stacking → shift hours to `interview-prep`; stalled at final rounds → debrief + `negotiation` prep.

## Warm > cold at senior level
Weight the plan toward `reconnect-list` / `network` touches — referrals convert far better
than cold applies, especially for director+ roles. A week of 3 real warm touches often beats 8 cold applies.

## The log format (append to `personal/accountability.md`)
```markdown
## Week of {date}
**Targets:** {N applies · M outreach · K follow-ups · prep/skill goal}
**Actuals:** {from pipeline + reported} — applies {x/N}, outreach {y/M}, follow-ups {z/K}, prep {…}
**Hit / missed:** {per target, honest}
**Blocker:** {the real reason a miss happened — capacity? avoidance? waiting on someone?}
**Win:** {one real thing — an interview, a warm reply, a hard application shipped}
**Next week:** {adjusted targets + the one move to unblock}
```

## Review tone
Lead with the win, then the honest scoreboard, then the ONE thing to change next week. The point is
a sustainable, moving search — not a perfect week. Eight steady weeks beat two heroic ones and a
month of burnout silence.
