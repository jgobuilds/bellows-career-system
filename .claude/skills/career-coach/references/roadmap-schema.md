# career-roadmap.md — structure

The durable output of the career-coach skill. Lives at `personal/career-roadmap.md`
(gitignored). Living document — re-checked every 6-12 months, why-first.

```markdown
# Career Roadmap — {name}
_Last reviewed: {date}. A living plan — revisit when the why shifts or a real opportunity appears._

## Goal (3-10 yr)
{The target role/level and the shape of the life around it — comp, scope, industry, pace, remote.}

## The Why (the anchor)
{What actually drives them, in their words. Impact / mastery / autonomy / security / mission /
team-building / lifestyle. Every recommendation below serves this.}
- **Tension to hold:** {any conflict between the goal and the why — e.g., "wants CDO but loves
  hands-on building." Naming it is the point; the plan navigates it.}

## Current Snapshot
{Where they are now, honestly: role, scope (team/budget/breadth), strongest signals, domain.}

## The Path {(pick a route)}
| Route | Next role | Then | → Goal | Rough timing | Trade-off (vs. the why) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — climb in place | ... | ... | ... | ~N yrs | ... |
| B — lateral to accelerate | ... | ... | ... | ~N yrs | ... |
| C — smaller co, bigger title | ... | ... | ... | ~N yrs | ... |

## Gaps to the Next Step (honest)
- **Experience / scope:** {what the next role expects that the record doesn't yet show}
- **Resume evidence:** {thin/missing signals the next level screens on; ties to profile [NEED METRIC]}
- **Skills:** {technical / leadership / domain genuinely required next}

## Acquire Next (prioritized — how, not just what)
1. {skill/experience} — via {stretch project / lateral / cert-that-gates / exposure}. Unlocks {X}. Serves the why by {Y}.
2. ...

## Jobs to Watch For Now (stepping stones)
- **Accelerators** (buy a missing experience fast): {title archetypes} → add to `TARGET_TITLES` in `personal/userconfig.py`.
- **On-track** (real progress toward the goal): {archetypes}.
- **Holding / detour** (comfortable but stalls the plan): {what to say no to, and why}.

## Milestones & Review
- Next review: {date / trigger}.
- Between now and then: {1-3 concrete moves}.
```

## Notes for the coach
- Keep **The Why** at the top and literally re-read it before every other section on a revisit; plans drift toward title/comp, the why is the correction.
- The **Path** table's job is to surface routes and trade-offs, not to prescribe one. Two or three routes beats a single line.
- **Acquire Next** covers the next step only — not all 10 years. A short, real list gets done; a 10-year skill dump paralyzes.
- **Jobs to Watch** is the hand-off to the sweep: concrete title archetypes the user can drop into `userconfig.TARGET_TITLES`, split into accelerators vs. holding roles so the pipeline can flag detours.
