# Age-proofing — strip the signals, never the truth

Older candidates get filtered on *signals*, not on their actual experience. The fix is to
remove the tells that trigger a bias screen while keeping every real date, title, and outcome
honest. This is the opposite of lying about a history — you're removing noise, not hiding
facts. If a change would misrepresent *when* or *where* the user worked, don't make it; surface
it to the user instead.

Run this pass only when it's relevant: a candidate with roughly 20+ years, or one who says
they're worried about age bias. It never overrides `personal/career-profile.md` or the one rule.

## Strip these signals
- **Graduation years.** Keep the degree and institution; drop the year. (Certifications with a
  currency meaning — recent, renewable — can keep their year.)
- **The full career backlog.** Lead with the last ~15 years in full detail. Compress older roles
  into a one-line "Earlier experience" list (title, company) with no bullets. Keep anything
  genuinely load-bearing for the target, even if it's older.
- **Dated language.** "Seasoned," "veteran," "extensive experience," "proven track record over
  decades," or "X+ years of experience" where X announces a birth year. Replace the tenure claim
  with the specific outcome instead.
- **Legacy-only tech.** If the profile's tools skew old, lead with the current stack the target
  actually uses (where real) and let older tools fall to a secondary line — never invent modern tools.
- **Old-internet tells.** An AOL/Hotmail/Yahoo address, an "Objective" statement, "References
  available upon request," a two-column 1990s template. Modernize the *format*, not the facts.

## Keep (do not touch)
- Real dates on the recent, detailed roles. Employment-gap honesty. Actual titles and scope.
- Anything the target role specifically needs, even if it's older — relevance beats recency.

## The honest line
You are removing *cues that invite a biased screen*, not the *evidence of a long, real career*.
If deep tenure is a fit signal the user wants to make (domain depth the role prizes), that's their
call — say so and let them decide. Never fabricate youth any more than you'd fabricate a metric.
