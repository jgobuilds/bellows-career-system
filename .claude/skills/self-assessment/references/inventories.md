# inventories.md — the exercises and prompts

Run these conversationally, one pass at a time; don't dump all six at once. Reflect answers
back, then narrow. Ground everything in `personal/career-profile.md`.

## 1 — Values (narrow to top 5–7)
Offer a list, have them pick ~10, then **force elimination** to 5–7. Sample set:
impact · autonomy · security · mastery/craft · status/recognition · belonging/team ·
integrity · balance/lifestyle · adventure/novelty · service/mission · wealth · learning ·
influence · stability · creativity.
- Killer question: "Recall a time work felt deeply right — what value was being honored?"
- And: "A time it felt wrong even though it looked good on paper — what value was violated?"

## 2 — Strengths (great-at + energized-by)
- "What do people consistently come to you for?"
- "What have you done that others found hard but you found natural?"
- Cross-check: which SIGNATURE wins in the profile required *this* strength? (evidence)
- Separate three buckets: **good at / great at / energized by.** Target the overlap of great + energized.

## 3 — Motivators (force-rank)
Rank, don't rate: **impact · mastery · autonomy · comp/security · title/status · mission · team-building · lifestyle.**
- "If two great offers differed only on {autonomy vs. title}, which wins?" (resolve real trade-offs)

## 4 — Work-style & environment
Quick spectrums (place themselves, with a why from real experience):
pace (steady ↔ intense) · structure (defined ↔ ambiguous) · social (solo ↔ team) ·
mandate (run/optimize ↔ build 0→1) · org (big-co scale ↔ startup scrappy) ·
place (in-person ↔ remote) · management (hands-off ↔ close).

## 5 — Energizers vs. drainers
From actual past weeks: "What tasks made time fly?" / "What did you procrastinate on regardless of skill?"
The drainers you're *good at* are the sneakiest career traps — name them.

## 6 — Non-negotiables & dealbreakers
"What would make you walk away from a great offer?" Comp floor, remote requirement, industries to
avoid, culture red flags, travel/relocation caps. → offer to encode as `userconfig.HARD_GATES` /
`PENALTY_LANES` and confirm the comp floor matches section 13.

## Synthesis prompt
Write a tight self-portrait: "{name} is at their best when {conditions}; driven most by {top motivators};
great at {strength+energy overlap}; needs {work-style}; won't {dealbreakers}. Watch the tension between
{A} and {B}." Keep it honest and specific enough that apply-pipeline and career-coach can actually use it.
