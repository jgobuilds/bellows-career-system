# playbook.md — question sets + the door-openers

Prep 4–6, specific to *this* person and what you're exploring. Never ask what's on their LinkedIn.

## About their path / role (great for a pivot)
- How did you get into {role/field}? What surprised you about it?
- What does a normal week actually look like? What's the hard part people don't see?
- What skills/experiences mattered most to get here? What would you have done differently?

## About the company / team (great for a target company)
- What's it actually like on your team — the culture, the pace, the leadership?
- What kind of person thrives here? Who struggles?
- What's the team focused on / worried about right now?
- (If a role is open) What's the story behind that opening?

## About the industry / market
- Where do you see {the field} heading? What should someone entering now understand?
- Who's doing interesting work? Who should I be following/reading?

## The two door-openers (end with these — they open doors WITHOUT asking for a job)
1. **"Given what I'm exploring, who else would you suggest I talk to?"** — the referral chain; how
   one conversation becomes five. Always ask for a warm intro to anyone they name.
2. **"Is there anything I should be reading, doing, or thinking about?"** — invites mentorship and
   keeps the door open.

## Always ask
**"How can I be helpful to you?"** — reciprocity turns a favor into a relationship. Sometimes you
can genuinely help (an intro, a resource); offering matters even when you can't.

## Follow-up log (`personal/informational-interviews.md`)
```markdown
## {name} — {role} at {company} · {date}
- **Via:** {how you connected}
- **Learned:** {key insights — culture, openings, the real story}
- **Referrals given:** {who they suggested → follow up + report back}
- **Owed:** {thank-you sent? any help you offered?}
- **Keep warm:** {next light touch + when}
```
